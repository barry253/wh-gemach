About West Hempstead Gemachs

West Hempstead is home to many gemachs — volunteer-run lending libraries that share equipment and essentials with the community, free of charge. This site brings them together in one place so you can quickly find who has what you need.

Each gemach is run independently by its own volunteers. Browse by category or search for an item, and you’ll be taken to that gemach’s page to see what’s available and send a request.
